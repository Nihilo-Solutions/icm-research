# L3: Style

* Plain American English, written for a technical founder and a non-technical marketing lead who will both read it.
* Sentence-case headings ("Hosting model comparison", not "Hosting Model Comparison").
* No em dashes and no en dashes. Use commas, colons, periods, parentheses, or "to" for ranges.
* Lead with the answer, then the evidence.
* Tables for comparisons. Mermaid for diagrams (`flowchart` or `graph`, no styling directives).
* Do not pad. If something is unknown, say "unknown" and give the next check.
* Name products exactly as the vendor names them on the cited page.
